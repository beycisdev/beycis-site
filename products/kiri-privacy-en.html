Kiri Privacy Policy

BEYCIS ("we") explains here how the Android app "Kiri" (the "App") handles user information.

1. Our basic approach

The App automatically cuts everything except the parts where someone is speaking out of your videos. Analysis, cutting and exporting of video and audio all happen on your device.

2. Your videos and audio

  • The videos you select, and their audio, are processed only on your device.
  • The App does not send your videos, audio or analysis results to our servers or to any third-party server.
  • If you select a video stored in a cloud storage service, that service's own app may download the video to your device. That is not a transfer made by the App.
  • We cannot view, listen to or store your videos or audio.
  • Exported videos are saved to the gallery on your device.
  • Temporary files created during processing are kept in the App's private storage on your device. As a rule they are deleted when saving finishes, is cancelled or fails. If any remain, for example because of the device's circumstances, they are deleted the next time the App starts.

3. Information we collect

We (BEYCIS) do not receive any personal information about you through the App.

  • The App does not obtain your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or similar information.
  • The App contains no advertising, no usage analytics and no mechanism that sends crash information to us.
  • There is no account registration.

However, the App includes a Google component for purchases. As described in "4. Purchase information", this component sends information outside the App, to Google. We do not receive this information.

4. Purchase information

The App sells a one-time purchase, "Kiri Pro", which unlocks exporting, through Google Play's billing system.

  • The purchase process and payment information (such as your payment method) are handled by Google. We do not receive credit card numbers or other payment details.
  • The App communicates with Google Play to process purchases and to check whether you already own Kiri Pro.
  • The Google component included for purchases (Google Play Billing Library) may send Google diagnostic information used to check that purchasing works correctly (such as whether an operation succeeded or failed, and connection status). Google handles this information; we do not receive it.
  • The App records on your device that Kiri Pro has been purchased. This record is not sent off your device.
  • Google's handling of information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Device permissions

The App uses the following device features for these purposes.

  • Notifications: to show the progress of analysis and exporting.
  • Background processing: to continue analysis and exporting while the screen is off or the App is in the background, including keeping the device awake during processing.
  • Internet access and network state: for purchases on Google Play, for checking whether Kiri Pro has been purchased, and for the diagnostic information sent by the Google component described above. It is not used to send videos or audio.
  • Google Play billing: to purchase Kiri Pro and to check whether it has been purchased.

Videos are selected using the selection screen provided by Android. The App never asks for permission to read all the photos and videos on your device. It also does not ask for permission to use the camera, record with the microphone, access your location or read your contacts.

6. Sharing with third parties

Because we do not receive user information, we have no information of our own to share with third parties. Information handled by Google in connection with purchases is described in "4. Purchase information".

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly receive personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may change this policy when necessary. If we do, we will post the updated policy on this page with its revision date.
